# Release 29C — Design Partner Engagement

**Gate:** I (Pilot)
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-22

---

## Goal

Engage 2-3 design partners for early, structured feedback on AdminMate AI. Validate product-market fit signals and refine core workflows before broader pilot.

## Scope

- Select 2-3 design partner companies (existing customers or warm leads)
- Define feedback cadence and structured interview protocol
- Provide white-glove onboarding and dedicated support
- Collect qualitative and quantitative feedback
- Synthesize findings into actionable product changes

## Required Work

1. Partner selection criteria defined (company size, use case, willingness to provide feedback)
2. Outreach and agreement (informal or NDA-based)
3. Onboarding session: walkthrough, data setup, first-use guidance
4. Weekly feedback sync (30 min) or async structured form
5. Feedback log maintained (feature requests, pain points, bugs)
6. Bi-weekly synthesis: top 5 findings, recommended changes
7. Exit: design partner phase concludes with sign-off on pilot readiness

## Non-Goals

- Signed partnership agreements or revenue commitments
- Custom feature development for individual partners
- Long-term support obligations beyond pilot window
- Marketing references or case studies during this phase

## Human Review Disclaimer

This document is AI-generated and requires human review before execution. Partner selection, feedback protocols, and NDA terms should be reviewed by product and legal. Synthesized findings should be validated against actual partner conversations.
