# Release 30E — 30/60/90 Day Review

**Gate:** J (GA)
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-22

---

## Goal

Conduct structured reviews at 30, 60, and 90 days post-GA to assess health, adoption, and roadmap alignment.

## Scope

- 30-day review: stability, adoption, early feedback
- 60-day review: growth, feature usage, scaling needs
- 90-day review: strategic assessment, roadmap update, expansion readiness
- Each review produces documented findings and action items

## Required Work

1. 30-day review: uptime, error rates, customer count, adoption metrics, top feedback
2. 30-day action items: prioritized, assigned, with deadlines
3. 60-day review: growth trends, feature adoption heat map, scaling bottlenecks
4. 60-day action items: prioritized, assigned, with deadlines
5. 90-day review: strategic health, competitive positioning, expansion readiness (Gate K)
6. 90-day action items: roadmap implications, resource needs, go/no-go for expansion
7. All reviews documented with data, not opinions
8. Executive summary produced for leadership

## Non-Goals

- Detailed sprint planning or feature scoping
- Performance reviews of individual team members
- Budget or financial forecasting
- Customer contract negotiations

## Human Review Disclaimer

This document is AI-generated and requires human review before execution. Review data should be collected from verified sources, not estimates. Action items require team lead approval. Executive summaries should be reviewed for accuracy and completeness before distribution.
