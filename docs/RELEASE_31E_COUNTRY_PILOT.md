# Release 31E — Country Pilot

**Gate:** K (Country Expansion)
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-22

---

## Goal

Run a small-scale pilot in the target country to validate end-to-end readiness before full country GA.

## Scope

- 2-3 local pilot customers onboarded
- Localized workflows validated in production-like environment
- Support and operations validated for local market
- Customer feedback collected and analyzed
- Go/no-go decision for country GA

## Required Work

1. Pilot customers: 2-3 companies in target country identified and onboarded
2. Localized experience: full country pack deployed and functional
3. Support: local language support or escalation path, SLA defined
4. Operations: monitoring, alerting, and runbook updated for local timezone/language
5. Feedback: structured collection from local pilot customers
6. Metrics: adoption, errors, latency, satisfaction in local context
7. Go/no-go: decision documented with data, risks, and mitigations
8. Retrospective: lessons learned for future country expansions

## Non-Goals

- Full country launch or public availability
- Sales team staffing for local market
- Partnership or channel development
- Revenue targets for pilot period

This document is AI-generated and requires human review before execution. Pilot customer selection should be validated by business stakeholders. Go/no-go decision requires leadership approval. Pilot findings should be reviewed for generalizability beyond pilot participants.
