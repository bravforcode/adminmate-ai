# Release 31D — Specialist Validation

**Gate:** K (Country Expansion)
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-22

---

## Goal

Validate country pack with local market specialists. Confirm functional correctness, regulatory compliance, and cultural appropriateness.

## Scope

- Local market specialists review country pack
- Functional testing with real-world scenarios
- Regulatory compliance validation
- Cultural and linguistic review
- Issue resolution based on specialist feedback

## Required Work

1. Specialist recruitment: 2-3 local market experts per country (paid or partnered)
2. Test scenarios: realistic admin workflows in local context
3. Functional review: does the product work correctly for local use cases?
4. Regulatory review: does the product meet local legal requirements?
5. Cultural review: language, UI patterns, and workflows appropriate for local market?
6. Issue log: all findings documented, prioritized, and resolved
7. Sign-off: specialists confirm readiness for country pilot
8. Feedback synthesis: lessons learned for future country packs

## Non-Goals

- Full regulatory audit or certification
- Long-term specialist engagement
- Marketing or go-to-market validation
- Pricing or packaging validation

## Human Review Disclaimer

This document is AI-generated and requires human review before execution. Specialist selection should be validated for expertise and independence. Regulatory findings require legal counsel review. Cultural feedback should be assessed for representativeness, not individual preference.
