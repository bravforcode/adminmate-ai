# Release 31A — Country Scorecard

**Gate:** K (Country Expansion)
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-22

---

## Goal

Define a standardized scorecard for evaluating and prioritizing countries for expansion.

## Scope

- Scoring criteria: market size, regulatory complexity, localization effort, revenue potential
- Weighted scoring model with clear thresholds
- Data sources identified for each criterion
- Initial scorecard populated for 5-10 candidate countries

## Required Work

1. Scoring criteria defined: market size, regulatory burden, localization cost, competition, revenue potential, technical complexity
2. Weights assigned per criterion (team consensus)
3. Data sources: market research, regulatory databases, internal analytics, partner input
4. Scorecard template created (spreadsheet or tool)
5. Initial scoring: 5-10 candidate countries evaluated
6. Threshold: minimum score to qualify for next phase
7. Top 3-5 countries identified for deeper analysis

## Non-Goals

- Detailed market entry strategy for any single country
- Regulatory compliance implementation
- Localization or translation work
- Sales or partnership development

## Human Review Disclaimer

This document is AI-generated and requires human review before execution. Scoring criteria and weights should be validated by product, engineering, and business stakeholders. Data sources should be verified for accuracy and recency. Country selections require leadership approval.
