# Release 31B — Market Selection

**Gate:** K (Country Expansion)
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-22

---

## Goal

Select 1-2 countries for initial expansion based on scorecard results and deeper analysis.

## Scope

- Deep-dive analysis on top-scoring countries from 31A
- Regulatory requirements documented per country
- Localization scope estimated (language, currency, date/time, legal)
- Go-to-market approach defined per country
- Final selection with rationale documented

## Required Work

1. Deep-dive: top 3-5 countries from scorecard analyzed in detail
2. Regulatory analysis: data residency, privacy laws, labor regulations per country
3. Localization scope: language support, currency, number formatting, legal requirements
4. Technical requirements: Supabase regional deployment, CDN, API localization
5. Go-to-market: channel strategy, partner ecosystem, pricing considerations
6. Risk assessment: regulatory, technical, market, operational risks per country
7. Final selection: 1-2 countries chosen with documented rationale
8. Stakeholder sign-off on selection

## Non-Goals

- Full regulatory compliance implementation
- Localization engineering work
- Sales team hiring or partner recruitment
- Marketing campaign creation for selected markets

## Human Review Disclaimer

This document is AI-generated and requires human review before execution. Regulatory analysis should be validated by legal counsel. Localization scope estimates require engineering review. Market selection decisions require executive approval.
