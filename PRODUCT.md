# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary**: HR Managers and People Operations leads at SMEs in Thailand, Vietnam, and Indonesia. They're busy, often juggling recruitment, payroll, compliance, and employee requests simultaneously. They access AdminMate throughout the workday — at desks, between meetings on mobile, and occasionally after hours. Their context is task-driven: they come to complete a specific job (review candidates, run payroll, approve leave) and need data at a glance without friction.

**Secondary**: Company Admins configuring settings, security, and billing. Managers reviewing team performance. Employees using the self-service portal for payslips, time-off, and profile updates.

## Product Purpose

AdminMate AI is an AI-powered HR platform that makes hiring, onboarding, payroll, and compliance fast and reliable for Southeast Asian SMEs. It exists because the HR tools available to SMEs in the region are either too basic (spreadsheets) or too complex (enterprise SAP/Oracle). AdminMate fills the middle: powerful enough to handle real Southeast Asian compliance complexity (PDPA, Thai SSO, Vietnamese labor law), simple enough that a 5-person HR team can adopt it in a day. Success means HR teams spend less time on admin and more on people.

## Brand Personality

**Professional. Modern. Trustworthy.**

Three words, not adjectives: think the tool-disappears-into-the-task clarity of Linear, the crisp confidence of Notion, the earned familiarity of Raycast. AdminMate is an enterprise-grade tool that feels fast and capable, not bloated or corporate. It earns trust through precision (clear labels, no surprises, every interaction confirms) and through reliability (fast, responsive, consistent screen to screen).

The brand carries a subtle regional authenticity — it serves SEA specifically, not as an afterthought but as the primary design input (multi-language, local compliance, local payroll rules). This is not a Western HR tool localized; it's built for SEA from the ground up.

## Anti-references

- **Generic Bootstrap-era dashboards**: flat gray sidebars, unmemorable icons, heavy card borders, no visual hierarchy. The safe placeholder anyone builds.
- **Salesforce/Oracle complexity**: information density without information design. Too many fields, too many tabs, too many clicks to do one thing.
- **Over-decorated SaaS**: gradient buttons, glassmorphism cards, decorative illustrations that add no information. Every pixel should earn its place.
- **The "SaaS-cream" monoculture**: beige body backgrounds, blue-and-white button, rounded-corners-everywhere template that makes 90% of B2B tools indistinguishable.

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears.** Every interaction, label, and animation serves the task. Motion conveys state, not decoration. If a user notices the UI before finishing their task, the UI is getting in the way.

2. **Precision over personality.** Consistent component vocabulary screen to screen. The same button looks the same everywhere. The same form control behaves the same everywhere. Consistency IS the brand for a professional tool.

3. **Confidence through clarity.** Every state matters: default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, empty, error, success. Each one tells the user what's happening and what to do next. No surprises.

4. **Regional-first, not localized-after.** Thai, Vietnamese, and Indonesian language support are primary inputs to layout decisions (text expansion, RTL considerations, date/number formatting). The design works beautifully in English AND in local languages.

5. **Density with breathing room.** HR tools are data-dense by nature (tables, lists, payroll runs). The design provides generous whitespace around the content that matters most, and tighter spacing for power-user views. Every density choice is intentional, not default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum. Aim for AAA where practical.
- Full keyboard navigation for all interactive elements.
- Reduced motion support — every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative.
- Screen reader support via proper ARIA labels, semantic HTML, and focus management.
- Multi-language support (EN, TH, VI, ID) with RTL readiness.
- Color contrast that works for common color vision deficiencies — never rely on color alone to convey state.
